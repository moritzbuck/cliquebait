# CliqueBait : detecting clades living at the limit (of the 95% ANI gap)

A 95% Average Nucleotide Identity is widely accepted as a threshold to operationally define bacterial species. It is surprisingly robust to observation and no good coutner example was known until a large amount of Prochlorochoccus were sequenced. The observation that some clades of Prochlorococcus broke this barrier made the development of a tool to find such clade both easier and a necessity.
